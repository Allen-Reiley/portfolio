### 2.2 Standard Case Study Template (`/projects/[slug]`)
Every case study follows this 4-step engineering and business breakdown:

```markdown
# [Project Name]: [One-Sentence Commercial Summary]

## 1. The Commercial Problem
- What business friction existed? (e.g., manual spreadsheet orders, fragmented customer experience, slow response times).
- Who was the end user, and what were the operational requirements?

## 2. System Architecture & Decisions
- Why this tech stack was chosen (Next.js, Tailwind, Supabase/Database, API design).
- Data flow diagram or component hierarchy.
- Security, state management, and asset optimization.

## 3. Technical Challenges & Trade-offs
- The hardest bug or architectural hurdle encountered.
- Trade-offs made between delivery speed, maintainability, and complexity.
- How performance (Lighthouse/CWV) and responsive UX were guaranteed.

## 4. Business Outcome & Delivery
- What works today in production.
- Measurable impact on customer interaction and administrative time saved.
- Live URL link with disclaimer on sanitized/demo data where applicable.
```

  Synthesized online business models, contractor arbitrage, and operational SOPs into a 75-page book. Recognized that business is ultimately an algorithm of repeatable systems.

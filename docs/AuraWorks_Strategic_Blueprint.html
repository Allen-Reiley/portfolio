💰 Target Unit Economics (Per $4,000 Deal)
Gross Revenue: $4,000  |  Contractor Milestone Delivery: $1,200 (30%)  |  Sales Rep Commission: $600 (15%)  |  AuraWorks Net Margin: $2,200 (55% Net Margin) with zero fixed payroll overhead.

The 3 Critical Operational Bottlenecks & Fixes

Vulnerability Core Failure Mechanism Tactical AuraWorks Fix
1. Sales Rep Churn Commission reps quit within 14–21 days if deals take too long to close or leads are cold. Use custom Python scrapers to feed reps pre-verified, high-intent lead lists. Provide objection cheat sheets and pay commissions immediately on invoice clearance.
2. The Contractor Quality Trap Freelancers ghost, deliver buggy work, or miss deadlines near launch. Implement 30/30/40 milestone escrow, enforce the 5-7 Day Buffer Rule (internal deadline well before client deadline), and maintain a strict Black-Box Interface (clients never contact contractors directly).
3. Generalist Offer Trap Selling "general web dev and marketing" leads to low prices and slow sales cycles. Package a High-ROI Productized Painkiller Offer (e.g., "Automated AI Lead Intake & Speed-to-Lead CRM Pipelines in 14 Days").
